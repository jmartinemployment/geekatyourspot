Three real options near you, cheapest first.

1. FIU Small Business Legal Clinic — effectively free. The FIU College of Law Small Business Legal Clinic provides free assistance to small businesses and nonprofits, services are free except a one-time $25 administrative fee, and it serves Florida residents only. You apply by emailing the director, John Little, at johnlittle@fiuclinic.org, briefly describing the help you need. The clinic reserves the right to decline any request. Worth a shot, but they may pass on a revenue-generating product, and law students move on a semester clock.

2. Palm Beach County Bar referral — $50. You get a 30-minute consultation with an attorney in your area of need for a $50 fee, which applies in all areas of law except bankruptcy, labor, medical malpractice, personal injury, social security disability and workers’ comp. You fill out a form on their site and they email you the attorney’s contact details in about an hour. Phone is 561-687-3266, with a separate Boca and Delray line at 561-451-3256.

Now the part that matters more than the price.

Don’t buy one review. Your packet isn’t a standard contract — a typical business contract review runs about $540, and that buys you a generalist reading your indemnity clauses. It will not get you an answer on the Florida wiretap question or the FCC port-out rules. 
ailawyer

Split it:

The contract itself → cheap generalist. Flat fee, $500–800 territory.
Three questions only → one specialist hour. The consent gate, the regulated-provider classification, and the emergency exposure. That’s where a felony statute and an unwaivable federal rule live.

And don’t hand a $50 consult 45 pages. Send the two-page overview and the numbered question list at the end of the compliance analysis. Ask what they’d charge to answer just those. The answer tells you whether they’re the right lawyer before you’ve spent anything.
