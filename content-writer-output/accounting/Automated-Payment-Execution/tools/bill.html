How can vendors or payees check invoice status or fix vendor account issues in BILL?

How do accountants or bookkeeping firms get priority partner support from BILL?

Accounting firms and BILL partners receive dedicated assistance through Accountant Care

Why is my payment delayed or still pending in BILL?

Processing times vary by payment type, such as ACH, check, or virtual card. Learn how to track payment status and resolve delays by visiting the BILL Help Center

How do I report a security issue or suspicious activity to BILL?

If you suspect fraud, phishing, or unauthorized access, report it immediately to the BILL security team through the BILL Help Center

What is the BILL network?

The BILL network offers a secure platform for digital vendor payments, eliminating the need for checks or sensitive bank details. Connect with your vendors to send fast and secure ACH or virtual card payments. Vendors gain more control and visibility, and you save time. Start with a risk-free trial to see if your vendor is among the 3.2+ million vendors in our network. Learn more on the BILL Payments page

How can I pay vendors through the BILL network?

You can pay vendors in three easy steps: Search for your vendor by name and zip code in our network, or ask for their unique BILL payment network ID. Connect with them or send an invite from your dashboard. Then, send a payment via ACH, virtual card, check, or international wire. For more details, visit the BILL Payments page

What is BILL virtual card?

The BILL virtual card service is a free, easy, and fast way to make payments using your BILL account. Vendors receive a single-use, 16-digit virtual card number for processing. Find more information on the BILL Payments page
