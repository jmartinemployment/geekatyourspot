Duplicate invoices can occur if payment processors in your LearnWorlds account are also connected to Quaderno. Ensure that only one connection is active to prevent this issue.

LearnWorlds collects billing details such as address, country, postal code, and city at checkout. Quaderno uses this information to accurately calculate taxes.

How does Quaderno track real-time tax rate changes across multi-channel sales pipelines?

Quaderno connects to your payment processors, shopping carts, and subscription billing systems like Stripe. Quaderno provides real-time alerts when sellers approach US economic nexus or international VAT and GST registration thresholds. This allows businesses to register before they are legally required to collect tax, helping them avoid penalties for uncollected back-taxes.
