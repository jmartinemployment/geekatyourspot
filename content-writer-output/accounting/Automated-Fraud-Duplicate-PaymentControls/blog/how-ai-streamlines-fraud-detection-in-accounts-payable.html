Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between fraud detection and fraud prevention?

Fraud detection involves identifying fraudulent activities after they have occurred, using various tools and techniques to spot anomalies. Fraud prevention, on the other hand, focuses on stopping fraud before it happens by implementing controls and measures to reduce the risk of fraudulent activities. Automated Fraud & Duplicate Payment Controls can help small businesses in Miami-Dade, Broward, and West Palm Beach counties address both aspects by using AI to quickly detect and prevent potential fraud.

How does the system stop sophisticated AI fraud and deepfakes?

AI systems use advanced algorithms to analyze patterns and behaviors that are typical of fraudulent activity. These systems can identify deepfakes and other sophisticated AI frauds by comparing them against known legitimate transactions and behaviors. While no system can guarantee 100% prevention, these tools significantly reduce the chances of fraud slipping through by providing continuous monitoring and adaptive learning.

Does real-time automation replace human financial auditing?

Real-time automation complements human financial auditing rather than replacing it. While AI can handle repetitive and time-consuming tasks quickly, human auditors bring critical thinking and judgment that are essential for complex decision-making. Automated systems enhance efficiency by flagging potential issues for auditors to review, allowing them to focus on more strategic tasks.

Why is the industry shifting from manual verification to cloud-native risk tools?

The industry is moving towards cloud-native risk tools because they offer greater flexibility, scalability, and efficiency than manual methods. These tools provide real-time updates and can easily integrate with existing systems, which is crucial for small businesses in rapidly changing markets like those in South Florida. By reducing manual errors and speeding up processes, these tools help save time and cut costs while improving accuracy and security.
