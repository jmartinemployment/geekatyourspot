Frequently Asked Questions

Which accounting platforms does Cash Flow Frog support?

Cash Flow Frog connects to eight platforms: QuickBooks Online, QuickBooks Desktop, Xero, Sage Intacct, Zoho Books, FreshBooks, Odoo, and direct bank feeds via Plaid. This broad compatibility ensures that whatever your business runs on, it is covered.

What if I don't use cloud accounting software?

You can manually import your bank transactions or historical ledgers via Excel or CSV files to generate an automated model without software. This flexibility allows you to build a forecast from your real bank history, regardless of your current accounting setup.

How often does the data synchronize?

Cash Flow Frog runs an automated sync daily, ensuring your forecasts are always up-to-date. Additionally, users can pull real-time, on-demand updates immediately after modifying items in their ledger, keeping your data current.

Is the integration read-only?

Yes, the integration is read-only. This means that adjusting transaction deadlines, editing expected values, or launching alternative experiments inside the app will never change or disrupt your primary accounting ledger.

Can it sort cash metrics by internal company tags?

Yes, Cash Flow Frog natively supports sorting by QuickBooks Classes, locations, departments, customers, vendors, and projects. This prevents mixed reporting when a company has multiple arms or offices, allowing for detailed and organized cash flow analysis.

How long are the forecast timelines?

Cash Flow Frog maps a live, rolling 36-month operational horizon. Users can toggle the resolution dynamically between daily, weekly, monthly, and quarterly views depending on how immediate their cash needs are, providing flexibility and detailed insights.
