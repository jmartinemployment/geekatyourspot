The process is riddled with inefficiencies that stem from the very nature of spreadsheets. Then there's the problem of manual data entry. Every transaction, invoice, and payment must be entered by hand, a process fraught with potential for human error. Manual entry also consumes valuable time, pulling business owners away from strategic activities that could drive growth.

Spreadsheets are also notorious for their lack of flexibility. As Maria's business grows, the complexity of her financial landscape increases. She might need to track multiple revenue streams, varied payment schedules, or fluctuating expenses. Traditional spreadsheets are ill-equipped to handle these variables dynamically. Each adjustment requires a tedious round of updates across numerous cells and sheets, increasing the risk of oversight and errors.

Furthermore, spreadsheets do not support the multi-dimensional analysis that modern businesses require. They struggle to provide the granular insights needed to predict future cash flows accurately. For instance, Maria can't easily simulate potential scenarios, such as a delayed payment or an unexpected expense, to see how these would impact her cash position. This lack of scenario planning leaves businesses vulnerable to cash flow disruptions.

Finally, the collaborative limitations of spreadsheets cannot be ignored. In a team setting, multiple users editing the same document can lead to version control issues and conflicting data entries. This can result in team members working with different versions of the truth, complicating decision-making processes and compromising financial integrity.

People Also Ask

How does the software pull live data into the spreadsheet without breaking existing formulas?

Clockwork provides a solution by automatically generating models from your transaction data, avoiding the need for manual formula adjustments. It ensures that your financial data is always up-to-date without the risk of breaking formulas, as it does not rely on traditional spreadsheet formulas. Instead, it uses its own computational models to provide an accurate and seamless forecast.

Can it handle multi-currency or multi-entity structures if our spreadsheet rolls them up?

Yes, Clockwork and Dryrun both support multi-currency and multi-entity operations. Clockwork offers unlimited consolidation and custom chart of accounts, making it suitable for businesses with multiple entities. Dryrun provides automated multi-currency forecasting with real-time exchange rate feeds, offering a consolidated view of liquidity across international subsidiaries.

How does the system account for off-ledger or un-billed variables?

Dryrun allows finance teams to incorporate non-ledger variables by layering offline projections over automated data. This capability supports the inclusion of sales pipelines, project budgets, and other operational elements that are not yet recorded in the ledger, ensuring a comprehensive forecast.

How do we build and compare multiple "what-if" scenarios without duplicating files?

Clockwork and Float provide robust scenario planning tools that allow users to create and compare multiple "what-if" scenarios. Clockwork enables instant recalculations across all forecasts when a driver is changed, while Float uses scenario layers that stack on top of the base forecast, allowing for easy comparison without altering the original data.

How often does the forecast update, and how long does it take to maintain?

Clockwork updates its forecasts automatically from QuickBooks or Xero, requiring minimal manual intervention. Float updates daily from QuickBooks Online and allows for manual syncs whenever needed. The maintenance time is significantly reduced compared to manual spreadsheets, as these systems automate much of the data synchronization and forecasting processes.
