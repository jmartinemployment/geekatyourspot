Frequently Asked Questions

Who should collect sales tax?

You need to collect sales tax if your business has a nexus in a state. Nexus can mean a physical location, employees, inventory, or even using local marketing affiliates or online ads. Economic nexus also counts, which is when your sales volume in a state passes a certain threshold. Each state has its own rules, so always check local policies. Once you have nexus, you should register and start collecting sales tax from customers in that state.

Do you have to charge sales tax for online sales?

It depends on your sales volume and where your customers are. Many states require online sellers to charge sales tax if you pass their economic nexus threshold. If you meet that threshold, you must register and start charging sales tax. Some buyers may be exempt if they have a valid certificate, and some online marketplaces handle tax for you under marketplace facilitator laws.

How do I get a seller's permit or sales tax number?

Once you know you need to collect sales tax, you can use Quaderno’s sales tax registration guides for step-by-step help registering in each state. After you complete the process, you’ll receive your seller’s permit or sales tax number.

What is the sales tax in my area?

Sales tax rates change from state to state and sometimes even by city or region. States set their own base rates, and local taxes may be added on top. The total rate can also depend on whether your state uses destination-based or origin-based rules. Always check your local and state guidelines for the most accurate rate.
