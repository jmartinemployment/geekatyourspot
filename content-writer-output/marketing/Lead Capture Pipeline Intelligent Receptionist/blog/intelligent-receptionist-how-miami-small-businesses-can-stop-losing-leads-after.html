Frequently Asked Questions

Does an AI Receptionist completely replace my human staff?

No, an AI receptionist does not replace your team. It takes on repetitive and routine tasks, like answering basic questions or handling simple intake. This lets your staff spend their time on more valuable work, such as helping clients and solving complex problems.

What happens when a caller asks a question that the AI doesn't know? Does it make things up?

The AI receptionist will not make up answers. If it receives a question outside its knowledge base, it will politely let the caller know and offer to have a manager follow up. The system logs the question and collects the caller’s contact details for a prompt human response.

Do I legally have to tell my callers that they are interacting with an AI assistant?

The safest approach is full transparency. It is best to use a clear greeting that lets callers know they are speaking with a digital assistant. This builds trust and sets the right expectations from the start.

How are AI receptionist tools typically billed or priced?

  • Fixed or flat monthly plans: You pay a set fee each month for a fixed amount of usage. This works well for businesses with steady call volume and helps with budgeting.
  • Usage-based or per-call pricing: You pay a platform fee plus a charge for each call or minute used. This is a good fit for businesses with changing or seasonal call patterns.
