Most have tried at least one of three approaches: live answering services, basic phone trees, or one of the closed AI receptionist platforms that come bundled with a virtual phone system. Each has its own strengths and real gaps. Lobbystack enters this field with a different model: open-source, customizable, and designed to let small teams control their own data and call logic.

Live answering services put a human on the line for every call. This is the gold standard for empathy and complex situations, but the cost scales directly with call volume. Even the leanest pay-as-you-go plans quickly outpace what most small businesses can justify, especially in months with a spike in routine calls. Operators follow a script, take messages, and sometimes book appointments, but they cannot adapt instantly to new policies or update intake questions on the fly. Many still leave you with a backlog of messages to return at the end of the day. Lobbystack does not try to replace the human touch for sensitive or high-stakes calls. Instead, it is built to handle the routine, repetitive work: answering common questions, qualifying leads, and booking appointments, so your staff can focus on the calls that truly need them.

Phone trees and basic voicemail systems are the fallback for businesses that cannot justify a full-time receptionist. Callers press buttons, navigate menus, and leave messages. This approach keeps costs low but frustrates callers who want a real answer now. It also leaves the business with the same problem as voicemail: missed connections and a pile of callbacks to chase. Lobbystack removes the rigid menu and the wait. Callers speak in their own words, get answers or book appointments in real time, and only get routed to a human when the rules say it is necessary.

Closed AI receptionist platforms, often bundled with cloud phone systems, offer a step up from voicemail. They answer in natural language and can capture caller details or route calls, but they come with their own tradeoffs. Most are proprietary: you cannot change their scripts beyond a few settings, you cannot see how they handle your data, and you are locked into their infrastructure. If your business grows or your needs change, switching platforms means starting over, retraining the AI, and sometimes losing access to your own call data. Lobbystack, by contrast, is open source. You can audit the call handling logic, change the script, and even run the system on your own servers if you want to keep all data in-house. This is especially critical for regulated industries or privacy-conscious teams. The open-source approach also means you are never stuck with a single vendor’s roadmap or pricing changes. You control the timing of updates, access policies, and retention windows.

Lobbystack’s pricing model is built for small business budgets. There is a free plan for browser-based testing, then paid plans start at $30 per month for 150 minutes and go up only as your usage does. There is no setup fee, and calls under 10 seconds do not count toward your allowance. This is a direct response to the runaway costs that can hit with per-minute AI or live services. If your business grows or you want to run Lobbystack on your own infrastructure, you can move to a self-hosted deployment with no additional software license fee, just your own server and provider costs. This flexibility is rare among AI receptionist solutions.

Integration is another place Lobbystack stands out. Many closed systems limit you to their own integrations or require workarounds to connect with your calendar or lead management tools. Lobbystack supports Google Calendar natively for booking, and its open API lets technical teams build direct integrations with other systems as needed. Businesses with more complex workflows or compliance requirements can configure the platform to fit, not the other way around. Where a live service or a closed AI platform would block you at the limits of their system, Lobbystack lets you own the logic and the data.

For teams who want to keep things simple, Lobbystack’s managed cloud hosting offers a turnkey option, no servers to run, just a dashboard and configuration interface. For those who need control or want to avoid vendor lock-in, the self-hosted option gives full autonomy. This dual-mode setup is not just a technical difference; it changes the long-term cost and risk for a business. You can start on the cloud, then move to self-hosting when your needs change, without retraining staff or losing your call history.

In short, Lobbystack is not a one-size-fits-all solution. It is designed for small businesses that want to automate the routine, keep costs predictable, and never lose control of their own data or call logic. Live services still have a place for complex or sensitive calls. Phone trees and voicemail are cheap but costly in lost leads. Closed AI platforms offer some automation but at the price of flexibility and data control. Lobbystack sits in the middle: more adaptable than voicemail or phone trees, more transparent and controllable than closed AI, and far less expensive than a live operator for routine work.

For a small team in Broward or West Palm Beach who has seen the pain of missed calls, Lobbystack is the tool that lets you keep your options open, your data in your hands, and your costs under control. That is the real difference when you look past the feature lists and focus on what will work for your business as it grows.

Answer these questions when booking your free consultation.

  • How many phone calls do you currently miss or send to voicemail each week, both during business hours and after hours?
  • When a prospect hangs up without leaving a voicemail, what is your current process for recapturing that lead?
  • Do you experience sudden spikes in call volume (e.g., seasonal demands, ad campaigns) that temporarily overwhelm your current staff?
  • What is the exact sequence of events that needs to happen when a new client calls to book an appointment or request a service?
  • Which calendar or scheduling platform do you use, and do you require the AI to write appointments directly to it?
  • What percentage of your inbound calls are just repetitive questions regarding your location, operating hours, pricing tier, or basic services?
  • What Customer Relationship Management (CRM), billing, or field service management software does your business rely on daily?
  • Are you looking to unify your voice calls with other communication channels, like SMS text messaging and website live chat, into one master dashboard?
  • Does your business require automated text-backs during a call to send digital intake forms, payment links, or address locations?
  • What is your target market's tolerance for interacting with automated voices? Do you cater to an elite or older demographic that demands a human touch?
  • Are there strict industry compliance guardrails (like HIPAA for medical offices or trust account rules for lawyers) governing how client information is gathered?
  • What specific criteria or emergency situations should trigger the AI to instantly transfer a live caller to your personal cell phone or an on-call team member?
  • Do you prefer a predictable, fixed monthly software bill, or are you comfortable with a variable pay-per-minute or pay-per-call model as your business grows?
  • How critical is total data ownership to your business? Do you prefer a turnkey cloud subscription, or do you want a proprietary system that your business fully owns and controls?

Frequently Asked Questions

What is an AI voice agent?

An AI voice agent is software that uses natural speech to answer calls and handle tasks like booking or messaging. It acts as a receptionist for busy small businesses.

Do I need to change my phone number?

No, you do not need to change your phone number. Starter and Pro plans include a dedicated LobbyStack number that you can forward your current number or overflow calls to.

What are the key features of LobbyStack?

LobbyStack provides automatic call distribution, call center management, dashboard reporting, and IVR workflows for inbound calls.

What is an AI dental answering service?

An AI dental answering service manages practice calls, schedules visits, addresses policy inquiries, and handles emergency routing to prevent missed calls going to voicemail.

Can it book new patient appointments?

Yes, it gathers contact and insurance information, books appointments directly onto your calendar, and sends a text confirmation.

Does it work after hours and on weekends?

Yes, it operates 24/7 to handle bookings, messages, or emergency transfers based on your rules.

Can it tell a maintenance emergency from a routine request?

Yes, using your custom criteria like flooding or gas smells, it screens issues and routes urgent calls to on-call technicians.

Can it answer questions about my properties?

Yes, it answers queries using details from your knowledge base and flags unhandled questions for the office.

Can it book showings?

Yes. Lobbystack syncs with your calendar, schedules slots, and sends confirmation texts.

Is an open-source AI receptionist free?

Live processing uses third-party services like Twilio and OpenAI. LobbyStack's Free plan gives you 30 browser voice minutes for testing.

How much does it cost and what fees or limitations are included?

Plans start with a free tier. The Pro plan costs $15 to $30 per month, which includes a set number of voice minutes. Overage and usage fees apply after that.
