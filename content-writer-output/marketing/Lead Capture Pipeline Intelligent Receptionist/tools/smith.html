Frequently Asked Questions

Is Smith.ai an AI bot or a live receptionist service?

Smith.ai combines both AI and live receptionist support in one system. The AI manages routine tasks like intake and scheduling, while over 500 trained live agents are available around the clock for more complex needs.

Will my callers know they're talking to AI?

Smith.ai lets you set how the AI introduces itself, so it can follow your disclosure preferences and any legal rules. The AI never pretends to be human. If you use the Virtual Receptionist, live agents answer from the start.

What happens when the AI can't handle a call?

If the AI cannot manage a call, it transfers the caller to a live agent right away. This way, callers do not get stuck or left without help.
