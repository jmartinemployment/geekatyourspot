How to decrease ACoS on Amazon?

Start by checking your search term report. Negative match any terms that spend money but never convert. Move your best-performing search terms into exact match campaigns so you can control bids more closely. On your product listing, make sure your main image and price are competitive, these affect conversion rate and can push your ACoS up even if your bids are fine.

What is a good ACoS percentage on Amazon?

A good ACoS depends on your profit margin and goals. As a rule of thumb, your break-even ACoS matches your profit margin. If your ACoS is below that, your ads are profitable. If you’re launching a new product, it’s normal to accept a higher ACoS to build sales, but keep an eye on your total sales to make sure organic growth is happening.

Do you want ACoS to be high or low?

Lower ACoS is better for profitability. But if you focus only on lowering ACoS, you might cut back on ads that help you rank higher. It’s smarter to track your total sales over time. If your organic sales are growing while you run ads, your ads are working, even if your ACoS looks high.

Why am I getting clicks but no conversions on my Amazon ads?

Either the wrong people are clicking your ads, or your listing isn’t convincing enough. Check your search term report for irrelevant queries. If the traffic looks right but still doesn’t convert, review your listing, price, main image, and review count are the usual issues.

What are the common mistakes in Amazon PPC?

  • Not checking the search term report often enough
  • Waiting too long to add negative keywords
  • Setting bids once and never adjusting them
  • Mixing auto and manual campaigns without a clear plan
  • Running ads on listings with weak images or few reviews
  • Focusing only on ACoS and ignoring other key metrics like impressions, click-through rate, and conversion rate

How to optimize an ad group with high spend and low sales?

Don’t just cut bids across the board. First, find out where the problem is. If you have plenty of impressions but a low click-through rate, your ads might not be relevant enough. Fix the root cause before making changes.
