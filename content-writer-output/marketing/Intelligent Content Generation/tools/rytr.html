Frequently Asked Questions

What is Rytr?

Rytr is an AI assistant that helps small businesses create written content and manage professional relationships. Its Create features help draft emails, posts, ads, and web copy. Engage brings together relationship context from your connected communications and helps prepare follow-ups.

What can you use Rytr for?

Rytr offers 40 writing use cases, such as emails, blog sections, ads, product descriptions, SEO content, and social media posts. It also helps prepare meeting briefs, track commitments, and draft messages for follow-ups with contacts.

Is Rytr a CRM?

Rytr positions itself as an AI assistant that works like a personal CRM that fills itself in. It builds relationship context from your emails, Slack conversations, calls, and meetings, so you do not have to enter data by hand. However, this does not mean it replaces a full sales CRM.

What is the difference between Rytr Engage and Create?

Engage focuses on relationships: who needs attention, what was discussed, and what you promised. Create focuses on writing: drafting and refining emails, posts, ads, and pages. Both use shared context about your contacts and writing voice.

Is Rytr free?

Yes. Rytr has a free plan that gives you 10,000 characters per month, one connected source, 25 active relationships, 25 Engage actions per month, three person-research runs, and a daily briefing. It also includes access to over 40 writing use cases and more than 30 languages.

How much does Rytr cost?

Rytr’s current monthly pricing is: Free at $0 for 10,000 characters, one connected source, and 25 active relationships. Lite at $9 for all free-plan features plus unlimited characters. Personal at $19 for unlimited characters, two connected sources, 250 active relationships, and 250 Engage actions per month. Pro at $49 for all connected sources, 1,000 active relationships, 2,000 Engage actions per month, and custom use cases. Yearly billing saves 20 percent compared with monthly billing and is charged annually.

Does Rytr offer unlimited writing?

Yes. The Lite, Personal, and Pro plans include unlimited characters per month. However, unlimited writing does not mean unlimited relationship-management activity. Connected sources, active relationships, Engage actions, and research runs still have plan-specific limits.

Can I cancel or change my Rytr subscription?

Yes. Rytr allows you to upgrade, downgrade, or cancel your subscription at any time. If you cancel, you keep access until the end of your billing period.

Can Rytr write blog posts?

Rytr supports blog sections, outlines, and other writing formats. Its editor lets you continue, expand, shorten, reword, and improve text, so you can develop and refine an article instead of just generating a single draft.

Can Rytr write in my brand voice?

Rytr says it can write in your personal voice or one of 22 preset tones. It also has rewriting tools to adjust wording, length, and style before you finalize your content.

What languages does Rytr support?
