How to decrease ACoS on Amazon?

Start by checking your search term report. Block terms that spend but don’t convert. Move your best search terms into exact match campaigns for tighter control. Also, make sure your main image and price are competitive, since weak listings hurt conversion rates and push ACoS up.

What is a good ACoS percentage on Amazon?

A good ACoS depends on your profit margin and goals. As a rule of thumb, your break-even ACoS matches your profit margin. Staying below that means your ads are profitable. If you’re launching a new product, a higher ACoS can be acceptable to build momentum, but track TACoS to see if organic sales are also growing.

Do you want ACoS to be high or low?

Lower ACoS is better for profitability. But if you focus only on lowering ACoS, you might cut ad spend that helps your product rank. It’s smarter to track TACoS over time. If your organic sales are rising while running ads, the ads are working even if ACoS looks high.

Why am I getting clicks but no conversions on my Amazon ads?

You might be attracting the wrong audience or your listing isn’t convincing enough. Check your search term report to see if the clicks are from relevant searches. If so, review your listing details like price, main image, and reviews to find where you’re losing buyers.

What are the common mistakes in Amazon PPC?

  • Not checking the search term report often enough
  • Waiting too long to add negative keywords
  • Setting bids once and never updating them
  • Mixing auto and manual campaigns without a clear plan
  • Running ads on listings with weak images or few reviews
  • Focusing only on ACoS instead of the metrics that drive it, like impressions, CTR, clicks, and conversion rate

How to optimize an ad group with high spend and low sales?

Don’t just cut bids across the board. First, find out where the problem is. If you have lots of impressions but a low click-through rate, the issue might be relevance.
