# Partner keyword research, 2026-10-21

The search queries people type into Google, with their monthly volumes, on the topics the technology partners cover, so that page titles can be matched to the words people use. Every keyword is an exact string DataForSEO returned on 2026-10-21 for a partner domain ranking in Google's top 30 (US, English), and every volume, CPC, competition figure and difficulty score is the figure DataForSEO returned for it. Nothing is estimated; a figure DataForSEO did not return is written as Not available.

**What is DataForSEO's and what is mine.** From DataForSEO: the keyword, monthly volume, the 12-month series, CPC, advertising competition, organic difficulty, DataForSEO's own intent label, the Bing and county volumes, the partner URL that ranks and its position, the People Also Ask questions. Mine, inferred from the words: the intent class (problem, solution, comparison, hiring, informational), the B2B confidence, the use case grouping, the pain point and use case sentences, the cluster names, the page recommendations and the suggested titles. The CSV files keep the two apart column by column.

## 1. Data coverage

- **Domains analyzed.** 105 partner domains from the GeekAPI `partner` crawl type (Mongo), crawled between 2026-09-30 and 2026-10-19. The list is in partners.csv with each run ID.
- **Sources and endpoints.** DataForSEO REST API, with the account in the site's .env.local, called from scripts; the credentials were read inside the scripts and never printed. Endpoints: `dataforseo_labs/google/ranked_keywords/live` (one task per domain, limit 200, top 30 organic positions, volume 30 or more, brand names excluded at the API for distinctive brands and afterwards for brands that are ordinary words); `keywords_data/bing/search_volume/live` (1,000 keywords); `dataforseo_labs/google/keyword_ideas/live` (two tasks, 200 seeds each); `serp/google/organic/live/advanced` (39 keywords, two per cluster); `keywords_data/google_ads/search_volume/live` (160 place-name phrases at US level, and 250 keywords at Florida, Miami-Dade, Broward and Palm Beach County level).
- **Retrieval date.** 2026-10-21. Keyword volumes carry DataForSEO's own update dates, from 2026-09-12 to 2026-10-15.
- **Geography and language.** United States, English. Local figures are separate, in section 5.
- **Search engine.** Google is the primary dataset (DataForSEO Labs). Bing is a supplementary column in the CSV files and is never added to Google.
- **Volume definition.** Google: average monthly searches over the last 12 months, as DataForSEO Labs carries Google Ads data; the CSV also holds the 12-month series, its median and the latest month. Bing: the monthly volume DataForSEO reports for the latest month. Google Ads county figures: average monthly searches for that location.
- **What was retrieved.** 16,401 distinct keywords across the 105 domains (200 per domain at most). 822 are partner-branded, 726 name another vendor, 1,460 read as consumer searches, 923 are form lookups, free calculators, logins, developer searches or searches for a person to hire. 2,595 are non-branded business keywords inside a use case cluster; after folding close spellings they are 1,383 head keywords, 581 of them with buying or comparison intent and 49 asking for a service.
- **Missing data and limits.** Bing returned a figure for 736 of the 1,000 keywords sent. The keyword-ideas expansion returned 1,000 keywords, but Google's category matching made most of them noise ("ap classroom" for "ap", "google doc" for SEO seeds); 49 passed the same filters as the main table and are listed in section 7, kept apart from the ranked data. The live Google results cover 39 keywords only. The ranked pull stops at 200 keywords per domain, so a partner's smaller keywords are not in the data. Two partner crawls (sovos.com, taxconnex.com) have status "external" in GeekAPI; their keyword data came from DataForSEO regardless. No AI assistant (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Copilot) publishes query logs, so nothing here measures what people ask those tools.
- **Spend.** $4.43 of DataForSEO credit for this run, against the $5.00 approved: google keyword_ideas 2 calls $0.14; google ranked_keywords 136 calls $3.61; bing search_volume 1 calls $0.09; google_ads search_volume 5 calls $0.45; organic live 39 calls $0.14 (31 of the ranked_keywords calls returned a field error at no cost and were repeated). Balance before the run $8.52.
