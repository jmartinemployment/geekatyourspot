The audit ran on 2026-10-20 against commit `45c8d3d`. It was run with the SearchFit SEO auditor agent and checked by hand afterwards.

- **Browser measurements.** Headless Chrome at 390 px wide with mobile-class network and CPU throttling, on local and live pages.
- **Hand checks.** These findings were confirmed against the code and the local build after the agent reported:

  - both robots files;

  - the 15 duplicated tool title groups;
  - the `[article-image]` placeholder on 14 posts;

  - the 80 `/glossary/crm` links on the Snitcher page;

## Launch gate: the X-Robots-Tag switch

**Current state.**
- All 432 URLs send `X-Robots-Tag: noindex, nofollow`, both live and locally. `next.config.ts` holds this until `ALLOW_INDEXING=true`.
- Per-page robots metadata does not contradict the plan. 431 pages say `index, follow`, and `/architectural-infrastructure-audit` says `noindex, follow` on purpose (`src/app/(site)/architectural-infrastructure-audit/page.tsx:15`).
- While the header is on, the stricter rule wins, so nothing is indexable.

**Before flipping it.**
1. Delete `public/robots.txt` (see H1).
2. Fix C1, so the first crawl sees a connected site.
3. Take `/architectural-infrastructure-audit` out of the sitemap (see M2).
4. Set `ALLOW_INDEXING=true` in Vercel for **Production only**, so preview deployments stay noindexed.
5. Redeploy. The header is written into `.next/routes-manifest.json` at build time, and `robots.txt` is prerendered, so the variable does nothing until a new production build runs.
6. Submit `/sitemap.xml` in Search Console. The verification meta tag is already on every page.

## Critical

### C1. Navigation and hub pages cannot be crawled from served HTML

**What is wrong.**
- The navbar mounts `SheetContent` (`src/components/layout/navbar.tsx:249`) only when the menu is opened. It renders one section at a time (`:293`, `:307`, `:321`).
- The footer (`src/components/layout/footer.tsx`) links only to the home page, Privacy, Terms, Facebook, LinkedIn and the phone number.

**Evidence.**
- Following links in served HTML from `/` reaches 419 of 432 sitemap URLs. The 13 it cannot reach:
  - `/blog`, `/tools`, `/tools/accounting`, `/tools/marketing`, `/use-cases`, `/use-cases/marketing`.
  - Seven blog posts that are linked only from `/blog?page=2` and `/blog?page=3`, whose canonical points to `/blog` (see M1):
    - `/blog/marketing/are-smart-chatbots-the-secret-weapon-for-marketing-success`
    - `/blog/marketing/smart-chatbots-for-marketing-transforming-customer-engagement`
    - `/blog/marketing/how-personalized-email-campaigns-drive-real-results`
    - `/blog/marketing/unlocking-the-power-of-automated-content-generation`
    - `/blog/marketing/unlocking-the-potential-of-automated-content-generation-for-small-businesses`
    - `/blog/marketing/unlocking-the-power-of-ai-for-content-repurposing`
    - `/blog/marketing/content-creation-workflow/automated-content-generation-transforming-small-business-marketing`
- `/tools` and `/use-cases` have no inbound link anywhere on the site.
- Glossary terms sit deep. 212 terms are at click depth 5 (home, then a content page, a term, `/glossary`, a letter page, the term) and 20 are at depth 4.

**Why it matters.** The hub pages tie the topic clusters together and receive no internal link equity. The seven posts can be found only through the sitemap, which is a weak signal.

**Fix.**
- Add server-rendered links to `/use-cases`, `/use-cases/marketing`, `/use-cases/accounting`, `/tools`, `/tools/marketing`, `/tools/accounting`, `/blog`, `/glossary` and `/methodology`.
- The empty middle footer column (`footer.tsx:80`, `col-span-6` holding only `&nbsp;`) is the natural place, and the navbar's behaviour stays as it is.
- The alternative is to keep the Sheet content mounted and hide it with CSS until it is opened.
- Then fix M1 so the paginated blog pages pass equity to their posts.

## High

### H1. `public/robots.txt` blocks everything and conflicts with `src/app/robots.ts`

**Evidence.**
- `public/robots.txt` is tracked in git and contains `User-agent: *` and `Disallow: /`.
- Locally, under `next start`, the public file is served.
- On Vercel the route wins, and live returns `User-Agent: *` and `Allow: /`.

**Why it matters.** Which file wins depends on the host. If the public file is ever served in production, it blocks crawling of the whole site. Before launch, it would also stop Google from seeing the noindex header.

**Fix.** Delete `public/robots.txt`.

### H2. The home page title carries no service or place keyword

**Evidence.**
- The `<title>` is `Geek at Your Spot | Geek @ Your Spot`, which is the brand twice.
- `src/app/(site)/page.tsx:204` sets `title: "Geek at Your Spot"`, overriding the better default in `src/app/layout.tsx:28`. Lines 236 and 261 set the same text for Open Graph and Twitter.
- Only 9 of 432 titles name a South Florida place, and the home page is not one of them.
- The H1 was fixed in `921d3b8`.

**Why it matters.** The home page is the best candidate for local commercial searches such as "AI consultant Miami" or "AI consulting South Florida", and the title is the strongest on-page signal.

**Fix.**
- Set the title to match the new H1, for example `title: { absolute: "AI Tool Integration in South Florida | Geek @ Your Spot" }`, and use the same wording on lines 236 and 261.
- The alternative is to remove line 204 so the layout default applies.

### H3. Tool page titles are bare product names, duplicated across pages

**Evidence.**
- 86 of 93 tool titles are only the product name, for example `Bill | Geek @ Your Spot`.
- 15 titles are shared by 32 pages:
  - "Bill" is used on 5 pages.
  - Melio, Ramp, AvidXchange, Tipalti, Cashflowfrog, Take the Helm, Quaderno, TaxCloud, TaxJar, TaxValet, Zamp, Hootsuite, Scalenut and My AI Front Desk are each used on 2.
- The H1 matches the title on each.
- The page bodies are distinct, with 8-gram overlap of 1% or less between same-tool pages, so this is a title and H1 collision, not duplicate content.
- The pattern is `const TITLE = "<Tool>"` in each `src/app/(site)/tools/**/page.tsx`, for example `.../intelligent-content-generation/jasper/page.tsx:19`.

**Why it matters.**
- These titles compete with the vendor's own brand page on navigational searches, which this site cannot win.
- They do not describe the intent the page serves, for example Bill for approval workflows.
- Google may treat same-title pages as alternates.

**Fix.** Title each tool page with the tool and the use, for example "Bill for Automated Approval Workflows" and "Bill for Accounts Receivable". Apply the same to the H1 if wanted.

### H4. Structured data errors that Search Console will flag

**(a) Placeholder image.**
- 14 exported blog posts carry `"image": ["[article-image]"]`, which is not a URL.
- The placeholder comes from export files under `content-writer-output/`, and the blog route injects `post.jsonLd` verbatim (`src/app/(site)/blog/[department]/[...slug]/page.tsx:66-69`).
- **Fix.** Supply a real image per post, or remove the property from those files. Do not substitute a generic placeholder.

**(b) SoftwareApplication nodes without required properties.**
- 320 nodes on 103 pages have neither `offers` nor `aggregateRating`/`review`, which Google requires for Software App rich results.
- **Fix.** Either make each tool page's main entity an editorial `Review` with `itemReviewed`, adding `offers` only where the pricing section supports it, or stop emitting typed SoftwareApplication nodes on hub and blog pages and reference them by `@id`.

**(c) Home FAQPage marks up hidden content.**
- The 4 questions in the home page's FAQPage node (`src/app/(site)/page.tsx:161`) appear nowhere on the page:
  - "What services does Geek at Your Spot offer?"
  - "Is the initial AI assessment really free?"
  - "What is the four-phase methodology?"
  - "Who is your ideal client?"
- This breaks Google's structured data guidelines and can lead to a manual action.
- **Fix.** Show the Q&A on the page, or remove the node.

The other 81 FAQPage blocks, with 846 questions, are all visible on their pages.

### H5. The glossary is thin at scale: 274 of 432 sitemap URLs (63%)

**Evidence.**
- Unique words per term page, after removing site-wide boilerplate, have a median of 41. 244 pages have fewer than 60 and 273 have fewer than 120.
- In `src/data/glossary/terms.ts`, 29 of 274 terms have an example and 24 have a category.
- 65 slugs are chat, messaging or support-platform jargon unrelated to the services, for example `whatsapp-qr-code-generator`, `telegram-chatbot` and `squarespace-chatbot`.
- Only 47 terms are linked from any non-glossary page.
- Term pages have no `og:image` and use the Twitter card type `summary`.

**Why it matters.** Site-wide quality signals weigh the share of low-value pages, and a majority of near-empty definitions dilutes the roughly 150 substantive pages.

**Fix.**
- Expand the 47 linked, service-relevant terms with an example, how the term applies to a small business, and a link to the matching pillar.
- Either remove the rest, or keep them out of the sitemap with `robots: { index: false }` until they are expanded.
- Removing them before launch costs nothing in search.

### H7. Titles shortened in `df3130d` lost their topic words

The shortening was deliberate, and this records what it cost.

**Evidence.**
- `/blog/accounting/how-ai-is-revolutionizing-tax-compliance-what-you-need-to-know` is titled "What You Need to Know".
- `/blog/marketing/how-ai-transforms-ad-spend-4-key-strategies` is titled "4 Key Strategies".
- The cross-platform chatbot pillar is titled "Local SMB Implementation Guide".
- The intelligent receptionist and lead enrichment pillars are titled "Streamline Lead Capture for South Florida Small Businesses" and "Smarter Lead Capture for South Florida SMBs". Both now target the same phrase as each other and as the Intelligent Lead Capture Pipeline pillar.
- Other titles lost "Multi-State Sales Tax Nexus", "Payroll Tax & Worker Misclassification" and "Intelligent Content Generation".

**Why it matters.** The `<title>` is the strongest on-page relevance signal, and these titles no longer name their subject.

**Option (owner's call).** Keep the short H1s and link labels, and set only the metadata `<title>` to a topical form, for example "AI Tax Compliance, What You Need to Know".

## Medium

### M1. Blog pagination canonicalizes to page 1

**Evidence.**
- `/blog?page=2` and `/blog?page=3` both have canonical `/blog` (`src/app/(site)/blog/page.tsx:23`).
- `/blog?page=99` returns 200 with page 3's content.
- Because `/blog` and `/glossary` read `searchParams`, Vercel serves them `private, no-store` with a cache miss, at about 160 to 300 ms TTFB against 125 to 170 ms for prerendered pages.

**Fix.** Give each paginated page a self-referencing canonical (`/blog?page=N`), or move to static path pagination such as `/blog/page/2`. Return `notFound()` for pages past the end.

### M2. Sitemap correctness

**Evidence.**
- All 432 URLs return 200 with self-referencing canonicals.
- The noindexed `/architectural-infrastructure-audit` is listed.
- 401 of 432 `lastmod` values equal the build time, because `src/app/sitemap.ts` uses `now` for static and glossary pages (lines 34, 38, 45, 53). The hand-coded blog post also gets the build time, not its own date.

**Why it matters.** Google ignores `lastmod` when it is not reliably accurate.

**Fix.** Exclude noindex routes. Use a real modified date, such as the last commit time or a per-page constant, or omit `lastModified`.

### M3. Social share images

**Evidence.**
- 274 glossary pages and 31 exported blog posts have no `og:image` or `twitter:image`.
  - The blog route's `openGraph` override (`src/app/(site)/blog/[department]/[...slug]/page.tsx:37`) replaces the root object, so the image and `site_name` are dropped.
  - The glossary term route does the same (`src/app/(site)/glossary/[slug]/page.tsx:50`).
- 122 pages use `/images/GeekAtYourSpot.svg` at 116 x 48 as the share image. Facebook, LinkedIn and X do not render SVG share images.
- Only `/`, `/blog` and `/architectural-infrastructure-audit` have a raster image: `action-figure.jpeg`, a 1024 x 1536 portrait that crops badly in a 1.91:1 card.
- `/blog` and `/architectural-infrastructure-audit` inherit `og:url` from the root layout, so it points at the home page.

**Fix.** Make a 1200 x 630 raster default. Set the image inside each `openGraph` override, or use `opengraph-image` files per route segment. Set `og:url` on the two pages.

### M4. Trust signals (E-E-A-T) are thin

**Evidence.**
- No person is named anywhere on the site. The home hero image's alt text says "action figure of me" without a name.
- `/about` and `/contact` return 404, and no email address appears.
- Blog JSON-LD authors are a team typed as a `Person` ("Geek At Your Spot Editorial Team", 17 nodes) or an Organization ("Geek @ Your Spot Editorial Team", 14 nodes).
- Tool metadata uses `authors: 'Development Team'`, for example `jasper/page.tsx:75`.
- Tool pages state prices, such as Jasper at $69 per month, with no visible review date.

**Fix.** Add an About page with the founder's name, background and photo, and a `Person` node. Credit that person as author on posts and tool reviews. Show a "Reviewed on" date on tool pages.

### M5. Local SEO entity details

**What is right.** Full NAP (name, address and phone) appears in the footer of every page with a `tel:` link, and it matches the home page's LocalBusiness schema.

**Gaps.**
- LocalBusiness appears only on `/`. It has no Google Business Profile URL in `sameAs` and no `hasMap`.
- The business name appears in four forms:
  - "Geek at Your Spot" (the schema `name`);
  - "Geek At Your Spot" (43 publisher nodes);
  - "Geek @ Your Spot" (titles and 14 publisher nodes);
  - "Geek at Your Spot LLC" or "Llc".
- Publisher nodes do not reference `#organization`.
- The schema `logo` is the 116 x 48 SVG (`src/app/(site)/page.tsx:30`), under Google's 112 x 112 minimum. `public/icon.png` (180 x 180) would qualify.
- `sameAs` and `twitter:site` use `@geekatyourspot`, but `https://x.com/geekatyourspot` returned 404 to an unauthenticated fetch. Confirm that the handle exists.

**Fix.** Use one exact business name everywhere. Add the GBP URL to `sameAs` and `hasMap`. Point publisher nodes at `#organization`. Use a square raster logo.

### M6. The footer squeezes the address and contact details on phones

**Evidence.** `footer.tsx:18` uses `grid-cols-12` with `col-span-3/6/3` and no phone variant. At 390 px the columns measure 70, 155 and 70 px, the blurb wraps to about 15 lines, and the address sits in a 70 px column.

**Fix.** Following the site's two-block pattern, add an `lg:hidden` footer block with stacked full-width columns.

### M7. Phone and desktop copies differ on two pages

Googlebot smartphone indexes the `lg:hidden` copy. The copies match on 426 of 428 two-block pages.

**Home page.** The copies differ by about 10% of their words.
- The phone copy lacks the desktop's "Accounting Systems" block, has a different accounts payable paragraph, and lists different tools.
- "platforms you already uses every day" is a grammar error, at `src/components/home/seamless-integrations.tsx:97` and `:119`.

**`/use-cases/marketing/ai-content-creation-workflow`.**
- The desktop copy renders a literal `Claude&nbsp assists` (`seo-blog-article-generation-section.tsx:160`).
- The phone copy has a subsection, "Leveraging AI for Diverse Content Formats", that the desktop copy lacks.

**Fix.** Sync the copies and keep both blocks. The duplicate H1s on two-block pages carry identical text, send no conflicting signal and need no change.

### M8. JavaScript weight on every page

**Evidence.**
- About 960 KB raw (288 KB gzip) of JavaScript loads on every page, including 40-word glossary pages.
- The booking `SchedulerShell` (react-day-picker, vaul and date-fns, in a 168 KB raw chunk) is imported eagerly on 428 pages.
- Geist and Geist Mono are loaded and preloaded on every page but used nowhere (`src/app/layout.tsx:3`, `:16`, `:20`), since `font-mono` appears only in commented-out code. This does not involve Sora.
- Measured live on slow 4G with 4x CPU throttling and a cold cache:
  - LCP is 2.35 s on `/`, 2.07 s on a tool page and 1.98 s on a glossary term. The LCP element is always the hero text.
  - CLS is 0.

**Why it matters.** Core Web Vitals pass today, but the home page has little margin, and the JavaScript mainly costs INP.

**Fix.** Load the scheduler with `next/dynamic` when it scrolls into view, and remove the unused Geist fonts.

### M9. Glossary links repeat on every mention

**Evidence.** On the Snitcher tool page, 110 of 131 links point at the glossary, and `/glossary/crm` alone is linked 80 times. These are hand-coded `<GlossaryLink>` elements in each section component.

**Fix.** Link only the first mention per page, in each breakpoint copy.

## What is working well

- **Status and canonicals.**
  - All 432 sitemap URLs return 200, with one absolute, self-referencing canonical on the apex host.
  - `www` gets a 301 to the apex, `http` gets a 308 to `https`, and HSTS is set.
  - Trailing slashes get a 308 to the canonical URL, unknown URLs return real 404s, and there are no broken internal links.
- **Titles and descriptions.** Every page has a title with a consistent brand suffix (the only duplicates are the tool groups in H3), and all 432 meta descriptions are present and unique.
- **Server rendering.** 1,199 of 1,238 components are server components, so all article text is in the initial HTML.
- **Images.** `next/image` serves AVIF, with eager, preloaded heroes and lazy body images. Every `<img>` has alt text and dimensions.
- **Core Web Vitals.** CLS is 0, and LCP passes under throttling.
- **Two-block pattern.** The copies are identical on 426 of 428 pages, DOM sizes are moderate (294 to 1,411 elements), and there are no conflicting heading signals.
- **JSON-LD hygiene.** The JSON is valid on every page, there are no dangling `@id` references, `safeJsonLd` escaping is used, and FAQ content is visible on 81 of 82 FAQ blocks.
- **Pre-launch robots design.** `src/app/robots.ts` correctly allows crawling while the header noindexes.
- **Local signals.** NAP appears in the footer of every page and matches the LocalBusiness schema.
- **Outbound links.** They use `noopener` and carry no affiliate parameters.

## Recommended order

   - Delete `public/robots.txt` (H1).
   - Remove the noindex page from the sitemap and fix `lastmod` (M2).
   - Set `ALLOW_INDEXING` for Production only, and redeploy.
3. **Titles.**
   - Set the home `<title>` to match the new H1 (H2).
