## Critical

### C1. Navigation and hub pages cannot be crawled from served HTML

**What is wrong.**

  - Seven blog posts that are linked only from `/blog?page=2` and `/blog?page=3`, whose canonical points to `/blog` (see M1):

    - `/blog/marketing/smart-chatbots-for-marketing-transforming-customer-engagement`
    - `/blog/marketing/how-personalized-email-campaigns-drive-real-results`

    - `/blog/marketing/unlocking-the-potential-of-automated-content-generation-for-small-businesses`
    - `/blog/marketing/unlocking-the-power-of-ai-for-content-repurposing`

- Glossary terms sit deep. 212 terms are at click depth 5 (home, then a content page, a term, `/glossary`, a letter page, the term) and 20 are at depth 4.

**Why it matters.** The hub pages tie the topic clusters together and receive no internal link equity. The seven posts can be found only through the sitemap, which is a weak signal.

## High

### H1. `src/app/robots.ts` overrides the owner's `public/robots.txt` on Vercel

**Evidence.**
- The owner's `public/robots.txt` says `User-agent: *` and `Disallow: /`. It was added on 08-04 (`c77932a`).
- On 08-20, `54889c5` deleted that file and created `src/app/robots.ts`, which serves `Allow: /`.
- On 09-04, the owner restored the file (`72cfd4a`) and commented out the route (`f38b1e1`). Twenty minutes later, `5f3f1de` ("content changes") uncommented the route again.
- Locally, under `next start`, the owner's file is served. On Vercel the route wins, and the deployed site returns `User-Agent: *` and `Allow: /`.

**Why it matters.** The Vercel deployment does not serve the owner's crawl rule. Every crawler is told it may fetch the whole site.

**Fixed 2026-10-20, at the owner's direction.** `src/app/robots.ts` now serves `User-Agent: *` and `Disallow: /` until `ALLOW_INDEXING=true`. At launch it serves `Allow: /` with the sitemap and host. `public/robots.txt` is deleted, so there is one robots source on every host.
