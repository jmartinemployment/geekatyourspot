# Reviews

Audit reference: GEO C3 in `plans/geo-seo-audit-2026-10-20.md`, CA1 in `plans/competitor-audit-2026-10-20.md`.

## The problem

The business has no reviews anywhere.
- **GEO C3, 2026-10-20.**
  - The Google Business Profile has no rating.
  - BBB returns 0 results.
  - No Yelp, Clutch or Facebook reviews were found.
  - `/reviews` and `/testimonials` return 404.
- **CA1, from the public Maps data on 2026-10-20.** The IT firms now selling AI nearby show:

  | Firm | Rating | Reviews |
  |---|---|---|
  | C&W | 4.7 | 95 |
  | Capstone | 5.0 | 73 |
  | CPT | 4.9 | 67 |
  | Nerd Teks | 5.0 | 46 |

  Of the seven AI-first rivals in that audit, only Gold Coast AI has a rating, from 9 reviews.

Why it matters. Reviews carry roughly a fifth of the weight in local map results, and AI answers about local businesses lean on review platforms. With none, the business is the easiest one in its market for an engine to pass over.

## What development mode means for it

Reviews don't wait for launch. The Business Profile is live now, whether or not the site is indexed, so every review helps from the day it's posted.

## What I need from you

1. **Ask every client for a review.** Use the Business Profile's review link:
   `https://search.google.com/local/writereview?placeid=ChIJ0_NMl_5-Y24RH4XyLq8Pg3s`
2. **Ask everyone, not only happy clients.** Choosing who gets asked is prohibited by Google and by the FTC.
3. **Tell me when reviews are posted,** so I can recheck the rating.
4. **Decide whether the site shows the review count** next to the booking button once reviews exist, the way C&W does (CA1). That's a site change, so it needs your yes.

## My recommendation

Recommendation:
- Aim for 10 reviews first. CA1 notes that ten would put the business ahead of every AI-first rival in that audit.
- Ask through Clutch as well, because Clutch profiles feed AI answers.
- Don't add AggregateRating markup about the business to the site itself. Google gives no stars for reviews a business marks up about itself.

## The plan once you decide

1. You send the review link to each client after each engagement.
2. When the profile has a rating, I recheck it from the public profile.
3. If you say yes to showing the count, I add it in visible text beside the scheduler's booking button, linked to the profile, in both breakpoint copies. The count is updated by hand whenever it changes.

## How I'll check it, and its status

- I recheck the public profile's rating and count when you tell me reviews are in.
- If the count is shown on the site, it matches the profile.
