# Content gap audit, 2026-10-21

This audit asks what the sites that compete with geekatyourspot.com publish that it does not, and which of those gaps are worth closing for a business with two departments, accounting automation and marketing with lead capture, that serves small businesses in Palm Beach, Broward and Miami-Dade. The site is pre-launch and sends `X-Robots-Tag: noindex, nofollow` on every page, so every finding is something to build or decide before launch.

## The short version

- **Your content competitors are national software vendors, not consultancies.** The searches for your own topics are held by Ramp, HighRadius, Avalara, Tipalti and Digits in accounting, and by Smith.ai, Synthflow and Weave for receptionists and reviews. National AI agencies rank for little, and the local rivals rank for almost nothing, so comparing the site with them overstated how complete it is.
- **Your pillars already cover the core topics.** The gaps sit next to them: the voice AI platforms people search by name, bookkeeping and month-end close, expense management, the plain words buyers use for your services, and the questions owners ask before they hire you.
- **Build these first,** in this order: tool pages for the voice AI platforms (CG12), the bookkeeping and month-end close pages (CG2), the services page (CG5, CG3), and a page for your free first meeting (CG4). The services page is spelled out in its own section below.
- **Your call:** free calculators, which carry the largest searches in your topics and the largest build (CG16).
- **Dropped:** industry pages (CG1). The use cases are organized by department, and an industry page narrows the audience instead of widening it.
